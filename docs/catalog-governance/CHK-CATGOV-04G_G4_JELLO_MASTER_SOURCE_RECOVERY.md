## Commercial assessment

- `jbi-con-azu`: three exact blue views, but all are 82x1047 or 63x629; the
  clip/costado alternatives change view only and do not solve source scale.
- `jbi-con-neg`: two exact black views, both 82x1047; no material resolution
  gain over the existing source.
- `jbi-con-ros`: one exact pink costado view at 63x629; no alternate exact
  source was found.
- `jel-zen-azu`: three exact blue views at 1181x83 or 1181x114; the costado
  view has lower effective height and is not a master improvement.
- `jel-zen-ver`: three exact green views at 1181x83 or 1181x114; same result.

The visual review treated product identity, completeness, sharpness, effective
detail, aspect ratio, and commercial scale separately. A geometrically faithful
candidate remains unsuitable as a commercial master when the source itself is
too thin or low-resolution. File size or an alternate angle was not treated as
proof of better detail.
